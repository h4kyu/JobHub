Local score

free

Every remaining posting gets a 0–100 score with no model: role type from the title, target-field and “software” keywords in the title and the first ~600 characters of the description, company reputation, and off-target title words. At or above hi it is a clear yes, at or below lo a clear no, in between it is ambiguous. A posting with no readable description is never a clear no (unless its title is off-target): it goes to refinement.

Refinement (triage)

tokens, capped

A small model (Haiku) reads only the ambiguous postings' title, company and opening lines and returns a 0–100 plausibility score, blended with the local score. Below {{ st.triage.min_score }} it archives the job. Jobs it hasn't reached yet keep their local score and are listed as “not refined”; nothing is hidden.

Deep evaluation

tokens, on demand
